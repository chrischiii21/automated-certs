Introducing Certify 🎓✨

Ever run an event and then spend the weekend typing 200 names onto certificates? Or have someone who never showed up ask for one? 👀

Certify fixes both.

📤 Upload your design from Canva, Photoshop or PowerPoint
📋 Add your participant list by pasting names or uploading a CSV
🔗 Share one link. Each person types their name, Certify checks it against your list, and they download their own certificate.

Not on the list? No certificate. 💅
Typed "jose dela-cruz"? It still matches, and the certificate says José Dela Cruz, exactly as you listed it. ✅

Rather send them yourself? Bulk-create every certificate as one PDF or a ZIP of PNGs. 📦

Perfect for workshops, webinars, school events, hackathons and fun runs. 🏃‍♀️💻

Free to start, no credit card. Try it without even signing up 👇
